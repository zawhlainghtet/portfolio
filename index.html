Final-year research and prototype system for autonomous MRT passenger-density counting, combining embedded capture, AI crowd analysis, cloud result reporting, and a 3D-printed train model.

Low-cost servo feedback for robotics education

Research work on modifying hobby servos such as MG90s and MG996R into feedback-capable digital or analog servos for educational robotics. The work connects low-cost hardware, feedback data, first-order filtering, signal mapping, and control concepts.

  • Servo feedback
  • RC low-pass filters
  • Discrete signal processing
  • Control education
