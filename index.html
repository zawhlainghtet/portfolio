Research

Low-cost servo feedback for robotics education

Research work on modifying hobby servos such as MG90s and MG996R into feedback-capable digital or analog servos for educational robotics. The work connects low-cost hardware, feedback data, first-order filtering, signal mapping, and control concepts for students learning industrial robotics fundamentals.
